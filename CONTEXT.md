# Watch Store Demo

A portfolio storefront for an invented luxury watch brand, built to show prospective clients what a premium 3D shopping experience looks like.

## Language

### People

**Prospect**:
A business owner being shown this site as a sample of the work they could commission.
_Avoid_: Customer, client, user

**Shopper**:
The imagined person browsing and buying watches on the storefront.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, visitor

### Catalogue

**Tolvik**:
The invented watch brand whose storefront this is; it does not exist outside this site.
_Avoid_: The client, the company

**Timepiece**:
A single watch offered for sale, with a name, price and specifications.
_Avoid_: Product, item, hand watch

**Hero Timepiece**:
The one Timepiece presented as an interactive 3D object; every other Timepiece is shown in still images.
_Avoid_: Main product, 3D watch, featured watch

**Colourway**:
One combination of dial colour and strap in which the Hero Timepiece can be shown and bought.
_Avoid_: Variant, option, configuration

**Collection**:
A named group of Timepieces presented together.
_Avoid_: Category, catalogue, range

### Experience

**Act**:
One of the three scroll-driven chapters of Home in which the Hero Timepiece moves: arrival, turn, Exploded View.
_Avoid_: Scene, chapter, slide

**Stage**:
The area of Home, held under the navigation, where the Hero Timepiece stays in view while the Acts scroll past.
_Avoid_: Canvas, viewport, hero section

**Still**:
A fixed image of a Timepiece rendered from its 3D model, shown wherever the Timepiece is not in real-time 3D.
_Avoid_: Screenshot, poster, placeholder

**Exploded View**:
The moment where the Hero Timepiece separates into its component parts so the Shopper can see how it is built.
_Avoid_: Breakdown, anatomy, disassembly

**Part**:
One of the seven named layers of the Hero Timepiece: crystal, hands, dial, dial base, case, crown and pushers, bracelet.
_Avoid_: Component, piece, mesh

**Pose**:
How the Hero Timepiece is held at one moment of the Acts: which way it faces, where it stands on the Stage, how it is lit and how far each Part has left its place.
_Avoid_: Transform, state, keyframe

**Clock**:
What keeps the Hero Timepiece's hands on the Shopper's own local time, ticking once a second.
_Avoid_: Timer, ticker

**Demo Checkout**:
A checkout that collects no payment and ends in a simulated order confirmation.
_Avoid_: Fake checkout, mock payment, test checkout

**Demo Admin**:
A set of management screens showing a Prospect how they would edit Timepieces and view orders, running on sample data only.
_Avoid_: Admin panel, dashboard, back office
